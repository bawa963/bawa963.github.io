Honk Tonk Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026. This policy covers the Honk Tonk game for Android, made by [DEVELOPER NAME].

The short version: Honk Tonk doesn't collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Your progress stays on your device.

What the game saves on your device

To remember where you are, the game keeps a small save file on your phone. It holds:

  • your progress: the level you're on, your coins, your boosters, how many levels in a row you've solved on the first play, and whether you've solved today's song of the day
  • your daily bonus streak: which day you last collected it and how many days in a row
  • your settings: sound, music and vibration

This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own, and it doesn't use the internet. The developer can't see who plays or how, and doesn't sell or share personal information, because the developer doesn't have any.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party services that collect data. Google Play, which you use to install the game, handles its own data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Vibrate, for short buzzes when cars honk and crash. Turn it off in the game's settings.

The game doesn't ask for internet access, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Honk Tonk doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that collects data, such as ads or analytics, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
